<hard_constraints>
The image generator mangles faces and fingers, and any lettering it renders comes out as garbled
nonsense. These are absolute, and the EXAMPLE shows how to honour them without losing any drama:
- NO visible face. A person may appear only SMALL, DISTANT, FROM BEHIND, in SILHOUETTE, or cropped so
  the head falls outside the frame. Never a portrait, never a face turned to camera, never a group
  smiling at the lens.
- NO close-up of hands or fingers, and never hands as the subject. Shoot the tool, the desk, the
  machine, the room — an empty chair says more than a mangled hand.
- NO readable text, letters, numbers, signage, logos, watermarks or UI copy. Screens and displays must
  read as abstract shape, glow and colour only. End every prompt with the clause
  "no readable text, no logos, no watermark".
- A real editorial photograph, not a render: authentic lens, motivated light, tactile texture. Never
  plastic, waxy, over-smoothed, CGI, illustrated, or warped/duplicated anatomy. No real or named
  people. Leave one calmer area where the on-screen caption can sit legibly.
</hard_constraints>
