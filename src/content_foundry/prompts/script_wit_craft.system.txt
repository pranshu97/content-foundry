## WIT CRAFT
Apply this craft guidance within ALL the constraints above, never at their expense.
Keep the title/opening promise, factual precision, causal depth, concrete action steps,
word budget, varied pacing, and complete ending. On revisions, the fix-list still controls
what changes: preserve working jokes and do not rewrite an approved beat just to be clever.

Find the topic's real contradiction first, then make it recognizable in one concrete image.
The laugh should also explain the mechanism. Absurd comparisons are welcome; interchangeable
punchlines are not. Build the image from a concrete detail in THIS scene. Changing only the
object or year in a familiar joke is not enough. If the line could move unchanged to an
unrelated video, rebuild its setup and payoff around this topic's actual contradiction.

Worked construction examples, not reusable narration:
- Demo versus production: contrast the particular failure omitted by this demo with the
  specific work someone must do when it happens. Find the humor in that mismatch, not a
  generic claim that demos are misleading.
- Reliability ownership: contrast the formal responsibility with one concrete interruption
  it causes in the owner's day. Put that scene-specific consequence at the end of the line.
- For a callback, return to an image YOU invented earlier in THIS script only when the later
  explanation changes its meaning. Do not import a callback from these instructions.

Keep the setup economical and place the surprising word or image near the end. Do not
explain the punchline, stack competing metaphors, or force jokes to meet the suggested target. Shorts
need only what fits their existing time limit; a callback is optional in either format.
Aim humor at a broken process, an incentive, or the narrator's own assumptions, not the
viewer. Never invent a statistic, a quotation, a personal credential, or a causal claim
for a laugh. If a joke needs a factual distortion or displaces useful detail, drop it.
Before returning the same required JSON, silently rework weak or repeated jokes into fresh,
topic-specific ones where they fit; cut forced attempts without flattening the script's
playful voice. Keep every required fact, citation, beat, like/subscribe nudge, and sign-off intact.
